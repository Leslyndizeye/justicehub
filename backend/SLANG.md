# Kinyarwanda vocabulary notes

Reviewed on 6 October 2026. These notes describe usage and guide retrieval; they do not establish guilt, a statutory charge, or current penalties. `legalVocabulary.js` implements the mappings. The original question remains available alongside the research wording.

## Casual typing

The user's example **"bimeze gute c"** is interpreted as **"bimeze gute se?"**, a greeting meaning "how are you / how is it going?" `shared/chatCasual.js` handles this user-confirmed spelling and closely related greeting forms (including "umeze ute c", "amakuru c", and "bite c"). It also recognizes English "how r u" / "how are u", and acknowledgements such as "okay okay gotchuuu" ("got it"). These contextual typing rules are separate from the sourced legal vocabulary below; they are not a researched dictionary of all abbreviations. A standalone greeting does not inherit a legal topic. A greeting followed by a substantive question keeps the full original request. "c", "r", and "u" are not expanded globally, and quoted text, code, URLs, numbers, and legal identifiers do not become standalone greetings.

| **gutubura**, gutuburira, natubuwe | Ubutekamutwe / ubwambuzi bushukana — scam, swindle; “natubuwe” means “I was scammed” in this context | The user's example is corroborated by [local reporting of a phone scam](https://yegob.rw/wagira-ngo-yabyize-mu-ishuri-iyumvire-amajwi-yumugabo-wagerageje-gutubura-ntibimuhire-maze-agatukana-ibitutsi-nyandagazi-amajwi/). [RIB's description](https://www.rib.gov.rw/updates/news/news-detail/inzego-zumutekano-zahagurukiye-ubwambuzi-bushukana-abagera-kuri-767-babufatiwemo) supports the formal fraud terminology. This is not “gukubita” (assault). |
| **guteka / gutekera umutwe** | Gukora ubutekamutwe — scam or swindle | [Reporting on a police-described deception scheme](https://umuseke.rw/polisi-yafashe-abahanuzi-bava-i-kigali-bakajya-mu-ntara-guteka-umutwe/) uses the idiom. Do not translate it literally as cooking a head. |
| **magendu** | Smuggling / smuggled goods | [Police reporting on goods brought across the border](https://police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/nyagatare-polisi-yafashe-abantu-bane-binjiza-magendu-n-abacuruza-ibiyobyabwenge/). Smuggled goods are not necessarily counterfeit. |
| **panya** | Unofficial border routes | The [same Police report](https://police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/nyagatare-polisi-yafashe-abantu-bane-binjiza-magendu-n-abacuruza-ibiyobyabwenge/) names these routes. Match only with border, route, import, or customs context. Do not map references to rats or unrelated paths. |
| **kanyanga** | Crude illicit gin | [Rwanda National Police](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/kanyanga-distillery-destroyed-in-nyanza/) explains this local drink name. Do not translate it as cannabis or copy a dated penalty without checking current law. |
| **urumogi** | Cannabis / marijuana | Used in [Police reporting](https://police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/nyagatare-polisi-yafashe-abantu-bane-binjiza-magendu-n-abacuruza-ibiyobyabwenge/). Preserve whether the question concerns possession, use, production, or sale. |
| **GITI** in media reporting | Payment conditioning news coverage / media corruption | [Transparency International Rwanda's 2015 study, pages 43–44](https://www.ombudsman.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/ombudsman/documents/Raporo_zikorwa_kuri_Ruswa/Media_and_Corruption_Rwanda_2015.pdf#page=44) distinguishes it from ordinary meeting transport reimbursement. Match only with media context; a tree, place name, or “ku giti cye” has a different meaning. |
| **gutubura imbuto**, gutubura umusaruro | Seed multiplication / increasing production | [Irembo's seed-multiplication service](https://support.new.irembo.gov.rw/is/support/solutions/articles/47001285906-uko-wasaba-uruhushya-rwo-gutubura-imbuto) illustrates a lawful use of the same stem. Agriculture and livestock context suppress the scam alias. |
| **akantu**, **kurya akantu** | Possible unofficial benefit / bribery euphemism in service context | A [Police report dated 9 January 2016](https://www.police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/umugabo-afunzwe-akurikiranweho-kugerageza-guha-ruswa-umuyobozi-wakagari/?cHash=3af0d63a2f7ff4ed3acc000a0ff9cde4&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News) describes a Mobile Money payment referred to as “akantu.” Alone, the word means a small thing. The mapping requires nearby service/official context and a payment or requested benefit; ordinary food and gifts remain distinct. |
| **kwica akanyota**, **gukanda amaguru**, **umuti w’ikaramu**, **inyoroshyo** | Possible service-linked bribe | The [National Itorero Commission's June 2014 integrity training guide, page 23](https://www.minubumwe.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=67579&t=f&token=30e4f719717b3e8efe52aa5f609acebecd408d4c#page=23), hosted by MINUBUMWE, lists these as names given to bribes. Match with nearby service and requested-benefit context. Literal thirst relief, leg massage, and pen ink are excluded. “Kwica akanyota” does not become a homicide query. This dated teaching guide documents usage; it does not establish current penalties. |
| **ikiziriko** in Girinka complaints | Allegedly demanded unofficial payment to receive a programme benefit | The [Office of the Ombudsman's account of its 9–12 June 2025 Rutsiro outreach](https://www.ombudsman.gov.rw/ibindi/rutsiroabaturage-barenga-11500-bitabiriye-gahunda-yo-gukumira-no-kurwanya-akarengane-na-ruswa) records residents' complaints about payments under this name. Requires Girinka and payment/request context; a literal rope or tether is excluded. Preserve the allegation rather than asserting guilt. |
| **gucucura**, yancucuye | Stripping someone of money/property; method unspecified | [Police reporting on a stolen car, 2023](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/polisi-yafashe-ucyekwaho-kwiba-imodoka-akayikuramo-moteri/) uses the expression for taking property; [Imvaho Nshya's 23 September 2025 report](https://m.imvahonshya.co.rw/rubavu-umwarimu-akurikiranyweho-gucucura-abaturage-nabarimu-akoresheje-uburiganya/) uses it for alleged deception. Search both theft and fraud; do not select either charge or a theft sentence without the method/facts. Requires money/property context and excludes emptying a bottle or glass. |
| **abazunguzayi**, umuzunguzayi | Street vendors / hawkers | A [Police report dated 24 August 2017](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/rnp-assumes-lead-role-to-get-vendors-off-the-streets/) explicitly explains the local term. This is a livelihood label, not a synonym for thieves. Search vending/market rules; the old report's fines are not treated as current rules. |
| **mayirungi**, miraa | Khat | [Police reporting dated 5 November 2018](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/mayirungi-a-silent-narcotic-destroying-lives/) identifies these names together. Distinguish the substance from cannabis and heroin, and a health question from alleged supply/possession. Current legal classification needs separate verification. |
| **mugo** in substance context | Heroin | [Police reporting dated 29 January 2021](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/kigali-over-340kgs-of-seized-narcotics-disposed-of/) gives this local name. Requires substance/use/trade/penalty context; a person's name or title is excluded. Do not copy dated penalty ranges from the report. |
| **muriture**, **bareteta**, **ibikwangari** / igikwangari | Local brew names | [Police reporting dated 6 June 2016](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/how-western-region-is-combating-illicit-drugs/) names these alcoholic preparations; the singular “igikwangari” also appears in the [RBC-hosted 2022 public-health study](https://rbc.gov.rw/publichealthbulletin/img/rphb_issues/RPHB%204%20%281%29%20V5.pdf). A local name is not proof of composition, present legality, or guilt. Preserve the particular drink name in the original question. |
| **gushikuza**, yanshikuje | Snatching property | [Police reporting published 11 January 2025](https://police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/gerayo-amahoro-abatwara-moto-basabwe-kubahiriza-parikingi-zashyizweho/) uses it for phones and bags. Requires nearby property context. Force, injury, and other facts may change the offence; this does not trigger the stored ordinary-theft penalty answer. |
| **kwiyitirira**, yiyitiriye | Falsely claiming an identity or role | A [Police account dated 22 May 2021](https://www.police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/kigali-polisi-yerekanye-abantu-8-bacyekwaho-kwiyitirira-inzego-z-umutekano-no-kwambura-abacuruzi/) describes alleged impersonation of security personnel. Requires identity/official-role context; theatrical acting is excluded. Authorised representation and an allegation of impersonation are different. |
| **gutera ubwoba**, **gukangisha**, **iterabwoba** | Threats or intimidation; the last term can also refer to terrorism | The [same Police account](https://www.police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/kigali-polisi-yerekanye-abantu-8-bacyekwaho-kwiyitirira-inzego-z-umutekano-no-kwambura-abacuruzi/) uses these expressions for coercion of traders. Personal intimidation must not automatically become a terrorism allegation. Preserve any demand, target, and stated harm; establish the relevant offence separately. Fictional entertainment context is excluded. |
| **ikimenyane**, **itonesha / gutonesha** | Favouritism / preferential treatment in decisions | An [RIB report on investigator training](https://www.rib.gov.rw/updates/news/news-detail/minisitiri-wubutabera-yasabye-abasoje-amahugurwa-yibanze-yubugenzacyaha-guhangana-nibyaha-bibangamira-ituze-niterambere-ryabaturage) discusses these alongside misconduct. Requires recruitment/service/official-decision context. Connections are not necessarily relatives; favouritism is not automatically a paid bribe. Check the decision rules and actual conduct. The page does not expose a publication date. |
| **gukingira ikibaba**, bakingiriye ikibaba | Shielding or covering for alleged wrongdoing | [Musanze district's indexed official title and excerpt](https://www.musanze.gov.rw/ibigezweho/inkuru-irambuye/nta-muyobozi-ukwiye-gukingira-ikibaba-abanyabyaha) corroborate this use. The full page could not be opened during review; the model receives that evidence limitation. Requires wrongdoing/evidence context and excludes birds/wings. Lawful defence or confidentiality must not automatically be called obstruction. |
| **kunyereza**, inyerezwa, yanyereje | Misappropriating entrusted/public funds or property | [Police reporting on VUP funds](https://police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/ruhango-abayobozi-binzego-zibanze-bakanguriwe-kwirinda-ruswa-no-kunyereza-ibya-rubanda-binyuzwa/) supports the fund-handling sense. Requires money/property context. The tax sense is excluded from this mapping; responsibility, ownership, and conduct still need establishing. |
| **baringa** in a paid job/project scheme | Fictitious or nonexistent offer/entity/post | [Police's July 2016 magazine, page 11](https://police.gov.rw/uploads/tx_download/UMUTEKANO__No_33.pdf#page=11) describes a promised job in a nonexistent project. Requires a job/project/entity context and payment/request context. Fiction and the place name Baringa are excluded. The old report's article number and sentence range are not current-law references. |
| **kwiba konti**, konti yanjye yibwe | Account takeover / stolen account credentials | [NCSA's 29 July 2022 guidance](https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/uburyo-bune-4-bwo-kurinda-amakuru-yawe-bwite-kwinjirirwa/) explicitly discusses account theft. Search account access and identity issues; do not answer using the physical-property theft baseline. Never request a user's password or one-time code. |
| **kwinjirirwa / kwinjirira** in digital context | Data breach / unauthorised access | The [same NCSA guidance](https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/uburyo-bune-4-bwo-kurinda-amakuru-yawe-bwite-kwinjirirwa/) includes accidental disclosure as well as attacks. Requires nearby data/account/internet context and excludes entering a room or house. A breach is not automatically an intentional criminal attack. |
| **imeli z’uburiganya**, imeli mpimbano, **phishing** | Deceptive digital messages posing as a trusted sender | [NCSA's 1 October 2021 email-safety guidance](https://cyber.gov.rw/updates/article/umutekano-wa-imeli-uburyo-6-bwo-kohereza-kwakira-no-kubika-imeli-mu-mutekano/) explains this mechanism. The observed wording is “imeli z’uburiganya”; the other Kinyarwanda labels are descriptive retrieval paraphrases, not quotations from legislation. Fishing for fish is a separate meaning. |
| **ibyuma** in drink context | Local name used for certain substandard alcoholic drinks | [Police reporting dated 8 August 2026](https://police.gov.rw/rw/amakuru/amakuru-ya-polisi/news-detail/news/hakajijwe-ingamba-zo-kurwanya-inzoga-zitujuje-ubuziranenge/?cHash=94c1dd1cbc41946122e91e8c1228119c&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News) records this use. Requires beverage context and excludes tools, metal, welding, doors, windows, and engines. The name alone does not identify ingredients or a legal narcotics classification. |
| **bituga ukwaha**, kabituga ukwaha, **ururimi rwa veterineri** | Possible unofficial service payment / bribery euphemisms | The [June 2014 official integrity guide, page 23](https://www.minubumwe.gov.rw/index.php?eID=dumpFile&f=67579&t=f&token=30e4f719717b3e8efe52aa5f609acebecd408d4c#page=23) lists the main expressions. Requires service and requested-benefit context. A literal animal tongue is excluded; these names do not establish a sentence. |

The colloquial usage sources are evidence of language use, not legal authorities. Legal rules require primary legal texts. The stored fraud excerpt is Article 174 of the Ministry's published 2018 text; later amendments have not been established by these vocabulary checks.

Add a term only after recording a usage example, source, formal equivalent, English gloss, and competing meanings. Native-speaker review is still needed for regional variants and idioms not covered here. These are documented examples, not a complete dictionary or a guarantee of model accuracy. Common words such as “agatike,” “umuhuza,” and “ubutumwa” also appear in the integrity guide's bribery list; they are not automatically mapped because ordinary tickets, mediation, and messages are common lawful uses.

Research distinguishes slang/idioms from everyday and formal wording; not every item above is slang. Police, RIB, NCSA, official teaching material, and local first-hand reporting provide usage examples. English equivalents are research glosses, not authoritative translations of a particular statute. Search-result publication ages are not used to date a document; the dated Police magazine, for example, is July 2016. This expansion checked page text and printed dates where accessible and explicitly identifies the indexed excerpt used when a full page could not be opened. Vocabulary references are not added to the chat's “Pages checked” list unless the browser actually opens them during that answer.

Further candidates are left unmapped where evidence or ambiguity is insufficient. The [Ombudsman's indexed 2007 report](https://www.ombudsman.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/ombudsman/documents/Raporo_z_umwaka/raporo_y_ibikorwa_by_urwego_rw_umuvunyi_2007.pdf) records regional bribery euphemisms including “ubwiyunge” and “ubusabane,” but their ordinary reconciliation/social meanings make automatic offence mapping unsuitable. “Gutema,” “gukata,” and “guhuguza” need further usage and native review before assigning legal search categories. A broad slang list copied without context is not used.

Ambiguous payment expressions use a 160-character window on each side of the occurrence. Both required contexts must be present within that window. Only a qualifying occurrence is rewritten; a later ordinary use of the same word, inline/fenced code, or a URL is preserved. Short punishment follow-ups can retain the preceding user's topic, including property loss, without treating it as a theft conviction.

Short definition or legal-meaning questions without the required context receive a meaning hint describing competing senses, without selecting an offence or changing the original question. For example, “ibyuma bivuga iki?” can explain metal/equipment and the documented drink sense. Specific phrases win over overlapping shorter matches, so “kwinjirira konti” is not rewritten twice. Offline tests exercise the meanings, exclusions, overlapping phrases, provider instructions, and retrieval; they do not establish native fluency or factual accuracy of live model replies.

Wrong-language translation repair uses the same contextual vocabulary on its draft to help preserve these meanings. These hints are not instructions to insert additional facts or penalties. Matching and hint generation run locally and make no extra API request; translation itself still counts toward the existing Groq quota.

`chatVocabularyGuard.js` checks positive contradictory definitions in completed drafts before they appear and again after translation repair. It covers scam-to-assault mistakes, confusing the named substances, equating smuggling with counterfeit goods, misreading border routes as animals, labelling vendors as thieves, literalising beverage-related “ibyuma,” Girinka payment complaints or media-related “GITI,” defining connections-based favouritism as a cash payment, and turning a personal threat complaint into terrorism. Each rule requires the relevant question context. Negation and qualified comparisons are preserved; code examples and explicitly labelled wrong quotations are excluded. Multiple detected conflicts can return several corrected meanings together.

If detected, the wrong draft and its browser citations are discarded. The replacement uses reviewed usage explanations and source links, with no invented penalty. Known contradictory assistant definitions are omitted from later model context without deleting the saved conversation. These are targeted pattern checks: unrecognised phrasings, reasoning errors, unsupported legal claims, and regional differences can escape them, and false positives remain possible. They are not a general fact checker or a guarantee of native-language accuracy. No additional correction request is made to Groq; ordinary wrong-language translation repair still uses the existing quota.

`chatCorrection.js` recognises corrective follow-ups in English, Ikinyarwanda, and French and retains the last substantive user question through repeated corrections. New explicit clarifications take precedence over the old interpretation. The prior answer is labelled as a disputed claim rather than evidence. The model should acknowledge a demonstrated mistake, explain the corrected meaning, and answer the revised question. A user challenge by itself does not prove an earlier legal statement false. Neither a user clarification nor a model correction automatically changes the shared dictionary or a user's saved memory.

## Sexual-offence terminology reviewed 9 October 2026

These are common expressions and legal terms, rather than a list consisting entirely of slang. They are meaning and retrieval hints, not a current consolidated statute or a source of sentence ranges.

| Expression | English meaning and scope | Reviewed source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **gufata ku ngufu**, typed **gufata kungufu**, **gufatakungufu** | Rape in sexual-offence usage. Person-object variants and some common inflections are recognised. Literal force during an arrest or taking property is a different sense; preserve the stated facts. | The [Rwanda Law Reform Commission legal dictionary](https://www.rlrc.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/RLRC/Publications/Dictionary_of_Legal_Terms/Dictionary_of_Legal_Terms.pdf) pairs this expression with rape and French viol. Its indexed multilingual entry was available; the full dictionary PDF could not be opened during review. [Police reporting dated 27 August 2015](https://www.police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/abagabo-babiri-bafunzwe-bakekwaho-gufata-kungufu/) confirms joined spelling in sexual-offence usage. Neither source's historical sentence ranges are adopted. |
| **gusambanya ku gahato**, **gukoresha undi imibonano mpuzabitsina ku gahato** | Rape / forced sexual intercourse. Statutory elements require the applicable text and facts, including consent. | The [Ministry of Justice's 2018 offences law](https://www.minijust.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minijust/Publications/Laws/Offences_and_penalties_in_general_2018.pdf), Article 134, and the dictionary entry above. |
| **gusambanya umwana / abana** | Child defilement / sexual abuse of a child. Preserve age and child-specific rules; do not treat this as consensual adult intercourse. | The same 2018 text, Article 133, uses separate Kinyarwanda, English and French headings. |
| **guhoza ku nkeke bifitanye isano n'imibonano mpuzabitsina** | Sexual harassment. Harassment without a sexual context is a different topic; this phrase is not a synonym for rape. | The same 2018 text, Article 149. |
| **ihohotera / ihohoterwa rishingiye ku gitsina** | Gender-based violence (GBV), a broader category than rape. Determine the actual conduct before identifying an offence. | [Police's Ngoma outreach report](https://www.police.gov.rw/media/news-detail/news/ngoma-abayobozi-bakanguriwe-kurushaho-kurwanya-ihohoterwa-rishingiye-ku-gitsina-nirikorerwa-abana/) discusses multiple forms. |
| **urukozasoni** when discussing an offence | Indecent assault in the reviewed legal text. Everyday references to shame or indecency do not establish an offence. | The 2018 text, Article 135. |
